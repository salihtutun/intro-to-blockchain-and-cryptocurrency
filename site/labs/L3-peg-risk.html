Three steps

  1. Open Lorenzo Naranjo, Stablecoins and Peg Risk:

https://www.lorenzonaranjo.com/class-materials/fintech/notebooks.html

  1. Run it and pick one fiat-backed stablecoin in the data.
  2. Answer below. Do not download a random web essay.

Questions (20 points)

Q1 (4). When did that coin’s price leave 1.00? Largest absolute gap you report? Screenshot L3-depeg.png.

Q2 (4). 5–7 sentences, class language: how the peg is supposed to work (mint/redeem or a second market), and one friction that can stop that (weekend banking, redemption gate, doubt about reserves).

Q3 (4). Why a Terra/Luna-style design fails a different way than a reserve-backed token that cannot redeem on time. Not a news recap.

Q4 (4). Your team coin: is it trying to be 1-for-1 CLASSUSD, a voucher, or free-floating scrip? What would a “depeg” mean for you? Three dated quotes or your A4 path → L3-ourcoin.png.

Q5 (4). Two kill criteria (if-then) you will paste into A6. They need a number or an operation (spread, depth, pause, wait time) — not “if we feel nervous.”
